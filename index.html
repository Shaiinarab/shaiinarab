I’m Shahin, a Computer Engineering student at Zand Institute of Higher Education in Shiraz, Iran. My current coursework includes linear algebra and fundamentals of algorithms, while I continue learning programming and web development outside class.

I’m interested in the way ideas become systems: how an algorithm is reasoned about, how software is shaped into a tool, how AI changes the possibilities, and how creative work can emerge from technical constraints.
